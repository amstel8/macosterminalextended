Extended Terminal 0.3.2

Copy on selection, keyboard editing and a dark profile for Apple's Terminal.

  • Control + arrows: start/end of line.
  • Control + Up/Down: beginning/end of the entire input; add Shift to select.
  • Option + arrows: move by words.
  • Option + Shift + arrows: select words in Zsh.
  • Control + Shift + arrows: select to line edges in Zsh.
  • Shift + Enter: insert a newline without submitting input.
  • iTerm Dark with Monaco 12 and all profile colors.

Settings are in System Preferences → Extended Terminal on Monterey. The background helper is stored under Library/Application Support, without a Dock or menu bar icon.

Grant Accessibility to the helper and allow Automation access to Terminal for the theme. Open a new Zsh tab after installation. The installer backs up your shell and theme settings.
